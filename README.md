# MTN Ghana — Sustainability Reporting & Financial Connectivity Analysis

A prototype sustainability reporting tool and a four-part analysis of how MTN Ghana's (Scancom PLC, Ghana Stock Exchange) sustainability disclosures connect to its financial statements, its integrated-reporting elements, and how its disclosure maturity compares to its global parent, MTN Group.

Built as part of an MSc Accounting & Finance corporate reporting capstone — included here because the underlying skills (sourcing and verifying real company data, building BI tooling on top of it, and writing evidence-based analysis) are directly transferable to data analyst work.

## What's in this project

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `Case_1_MTN_Ghana_Prototype_Tool.pbix` | Power BI dashboard — 5 pages: Executive Sustainability Overview, Doing for Planet, Doing for People, Doing It Right, Targets & Financial Connections |

## Data sources

All figures are drawn from MTN Ghana's own published Annual Reports (2023–2025), its 2025 Financial Report, its 2026 AGM communication, and MTN Group's FY2025 Sustainability Report. Nothing is estimated or invented — where a figure wasn't publicly disclosed, it's marked "Not disclosed" rather than filled in. Full citations are in each document's References section.

## Key finding

MTN Ghana's 2025 disclosed tax figure (GHS 10.5 billion) is an **exact independent match** against its own audited Financial Report — one of the clearest examples of "Direct" sustainability-to-financial-statement connectivity found in the analysis. By contrast, most Environmental and Social KPIs remain narrative-only, with no separately disclosed monetary figure — a pattern that also shows up when compared against MTN Group's more mature, independently assured, Scope 1–3 reporting.

## Tools used

Power BI (DAX measures, dynamic SWITCH-based KPI tables), Excel, Python (data sourcing and verification), Word (case-study writeups, APA referencing).

## Methodology note

Case 1's KPI targets are limited to what MTN Ghana and MTN Group have actually publicly committed to (50% emissions reduction by 2030; net-zero by 2040) — no target was invented for KPIs without a disclosed commitment, which is itself a finding discussed.
